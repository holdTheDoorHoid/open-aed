# Letter 4: confirming a dataset licence

**To:** the corresponding author of the "Cardially" dataset (Mendeley Data, University of Brescia)
**Subject:** Licence of the Cardially dataset

*Find the corresponding author's address on the dataset page: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/wpr5nzyn2z/1*

---

Dear Dr [surname],

Thank you for publishing the Cardially dataset of pre-shock and post-shock recordings. It is one of very few open collections of real out-of-hospital arrest recordings.

I lead an open source project developing defibrillator rhythm-analysis software, published under share-alike licences. I would like to use your dataset to test it and want to be sure I do so within your terms.

The Mendeley Data page shows the licence as CC BY 4.0. I have seen the companion article described elsewhere as CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Could you confirm which licence applies to the recordings themselves, and whether you are content for:

1. the recordings to be used to evaluate open source software, with the dataset cited as you request, and
2. algorithm settings derived partly from the recordings to be published under an open source licence?

I would not redistribute the recordings.

If you would be interested in the results, I would be glad to send them.

With thanks,
